4D Speed Momentum Analysis

Explore the relationship between Previous Speed, Current Speed, Time Since Candle Open, and Net Profit.

X-Axis: Prev Candle Speed | Y-Axis: Curr Candle Speed | Z-Axis: Time Since Open (sec) | Color: Net Profit

Cluster 1: Early Fakeouts (Losses)

Location: Low Z (0-10 sec), High Y (Current Speed), Low X (Prev Speed).

Why: The previous candle was dead. Suddenly, at the open of a new candle, there's a huge speed spike. Because the time since open is so small, 1 or 2 fast ticks make the "average speed" look massive. It's usually noise or a stop hunt, trapping early breakout traders.

Cluster 2: True Momentum (Wins)

Location: Mid Z (15-45 sec), High Y (Current Speed), High X (Prev Speed).

Why: The previous candle already established sustained momentum. The current candle is 15+ seconds in and is STILL maintaining high speed. This indicates real, sustained institutional volume pushing the market. These are the golden setups.

Cluster 3: Exhaustion (Mixed/Losses)

Location: Any Z, Low Y (Current Speed), High X (Prev Speed).

Why: The previous candle was moving extremely fast. Now, the current candle's speed has dropped off significantly. Entering here means you are likely buying the top or selling the bottom right before a pullback. Results are mixed to negative.

Cluster 4: Random Noise

Location: Scattered across middle values.

Why: Average previous speed, average current speed. Choppy conditions resulting in break-even noise or spread out results.
