Input Cluster Pathways (Parallel Coordinates)

💡 How to use Parallel Coordinates

This chart maps every single optimization pass as a single line flowing from left to right across all your inputs and ending at the outputs (like Profit).
To find the best clusters: Click and drag vertically on any axis (like Profit) to select only the top performing passes. The chart will instantly fade out bad passes, revealing the exact path (combination of inputs) that leads to those high profits. You can drag on multiple axes to filter even further.

Feature Importance (Correlation to Profit)

💡 What is Pearson Correlation?

Pearson Correlation measures the linear relationship between two variables, scoring from -1.0 to 1.0.
• +1.0 (Green): Perfect positive relationship. As the input increases, Profit increases.
• -1.0 (Red): Perfect negative relationship. As the input increases, Profit decreases.
• Near 0: No linear relationship. The input might be irrelevant, or its relationship is non-linear (e.g., the best value is in the middle).

💡 How to explore variables

Choose any two inputs for the X and Y axes to see how they interact. The color of the dots represents the outcome (e.g., Profit). Look for clusters of bright green dots to find the "sweet spot" ranges for those two variables combined.
