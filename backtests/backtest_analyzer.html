Monte Carlo Equity Curves (... Paths)

Visualizes possible outcomes of your strategy by shuffling and resampling your trades with replacement. Helps reveal if your chronological backtest result was just lucky (e.g. all your winning streaks happened together).

Monte Carlo Final P/L Distribution (... Sims)

A bell curve (histogram) showing where your final account balance ends up across all simulations. A reliable edge will have the entire cluster of bars shifted far out into the positive (green) territory.
